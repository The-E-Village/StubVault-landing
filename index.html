Built for collectors

Everything your vault needs.

Beautiful Digital Stubs
Every entry becomes a richly designed ticket stub. Artist, venue, date, seat, and category — all rendered in StubVault's signature style.
Your Album View
A stunning grid of every stub you've ever saved. Browse by year, category, or artist. Your entire history, beautifully organized.
Memory Notes
Attach a personal note to any stub. What you felt. Who you were with. The moment the lights went dark. The detail that makes it yours.
Your Timeline
Stubs sorted chronologically so you can trace your life through the shows you attended. From your first concert to last night's game.
Category Tags
Concerts, sports, theater, comedy, festivals, film, and more. Filter your vault any way you like. Your collection, your rules.
Locked In Forever
Your collection is stored securely in the cloud. Phone dies, phone changes — your vault is always right where you left it.
